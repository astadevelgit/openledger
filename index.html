How we sourced this

  1. Appropriation line items are normalised to a common MDA taxonomy, then reconciled against releases.
  2. Contract awards are deduplicated by project reference and matched to the awarding ministry and state of delivery.
  3. Contractor identities are resolved to a single entity; beneficial-ownership records are marked missing when no filing exists.
  4. Per-capita figures use 2022 population estimates and are shown for comparison only, not as an allocation formula.
